# Site Atlas — User Guide

## What is Site Atlas?

Site Atlas helps you track where you place your insulin pump infusion sets and CGM sensors on your body. Proper site rotation prevents tissue damage (lipohypertrophy) that can affect insulin absorption and sensor accuracy.

## Why Site Rotation Matters

- Reusing the same spot causes scar tissue buildup
- Scarred tissue absorbs insulin unpredictably
- CGM sensors in overused areas give less accurate readings
- Rotating sites keeps your skin healthy and your diabetes management effective

## How to Enable

1. Open Loop → Settings
2. Scroll to **Site Atlas** (orange map pin icon)
3. Toggle **Site Atlas** on

## Logging a Site

### Manual Logging (anytime)

1. Go to Settings → Site Atlas
2. Tap **Log Pump Site** or **Log Sensor Site**
3. Switch between Front and Back using the segmented picker
4. Tap on the body map where you placed your site
5. Touch and drag the pin to adjust — it grows large so you can see exactly where you're placing it
6. Watch the pin color as you drag: **green** means you're in a safe spot, **red** means you're too close to a recent site
7. You'll also see your existing sites shown as faded icons for reference
8. The pin will snap to the body if you accidentally drop it outside the outline
9. Add optional notes (e.g., "left of belly button")
10. Tap **Save Site** (always visible at the bottom of the screen)

### Prompted Logging (automatic)

When you deactivate a pump through Loop, Site Atlas will automatically ask you to log where your new site is. You can:
- **Save** — log the site location
- **Skip** — dismiss without logging

## Understanding the Color System

Pins on the body map are color-coded by age:

- **Red** — Just placed (0-4 days). Avoid this area.
- **Orange** — Still healing (5-9 days). Not ready yet.
- **Yellow** — Almost ready (10-13 days).
- **Green** — Safe to reuse (14+ days). Go here next!

The legend at the bottom of the map shows the full color scale from "Recent" to "Safe."

## Next Up — Finding Your Best Site

The **Next Up** section at the top of Site Atlas answers the key question: "Where should I put my next site?"

- Sites are sorted **oldest first** — the top entry is your best choice
- Each entry shows how many days ago it was placed
- Entries marked **Ready** (3+ days) are safe to consider
- Green-colored entries are your best candidates

## Adjusting Pin Positions

In the Settings body map, you can touch and drag any existing pin to move it:
- Touch a pin — it expands dramatically so you can see what you've grabbed
- Drag it to the correct position
- Release — it snaps into place and saves automatically
- If you drag it outside the body outline, it snaps back to the nearest valid spot

## Editing Entries

Tap any entry in the History section to edit:
- **Date & Time** — Correct when the site was placed
- **Site Type** — Change between pump and sensor
- **Notes** — Add or update notes

## Hiding Unused Locations

If you no longer use certain body areas, you can hide their entries so they don't clutter your map or Next Up list:

1. Find the entry in History
2. **Swipe right** on it → tap **Hide**
3. The entry moves to the "Hidden" section at the bottom

To bring it back:
1. Scroll to the **Hidden** section
2. **Swipe right** on the entry → tap **Show**

Hidden entries are not deleted — they're just excluded from the map and recommendations.

## Deleting Entries

- **Swipe left** on any entry to delete it permanently
- Use **Delete All Site Data** at the bottom to clear everything

## Tips for Good Rotation

- Wait at least 2-3 days between using the same spot
- Move at least 1 inch (2.5 cm) from previous sites
- Rotate through multiple body areas: abdomen, thighs, upper arms, lower back
- Check the **Next Up** list — green entries at the top are your best candidates
- Log every site change — even when prompted — to build a complete history
- Hide locations you've stopped using to keep the map clean

## Data Storage

- All data is stored locally on your device
- Site history is kept for 365 days, then automatically removed
- Use "Delete All Site Data" in settings to clear everything
- Data is NOT synced to iCloud or any external service
